Built for playing, not scrolling

Chords above lyrics

Write ChordPro, paste Ultimate-Guitar sheets, or use the chord toolbar. Auto-detection handles both bracket and plain formats.

Transpose anything

Shift from −11 to +11 semitones with the key kept readable, then zoom the text and let the sheet scroll itself.

Chord diagrams

Tap any chord for guitar or ukulele voicings, or open every chord in the song at once.

Set lists

Group songs, drag them into running order, and copy the list when someone asks what you played.

Share with a link

Send a song or set list as a link or QR code. Recipients can open it in the app or read it on the web.

Offline first

Songs live in a local database on your device. No account is needed, and nothing leaves the phone unless you share it.

Installing the Android build

  1. Download the APK with the button above.
  2. Open the file from your browser's downloads, and allow installs from this source if Android asks.
  3. ChordPlane appears in your launcher as ChordPlane. Future builds install straight over the old one and keep your songs.

The download is a signed release APK (not a Play Store link), so Android may show the standard “unknown app” prompt. Minimum version is Android 7.0 (API 24).
